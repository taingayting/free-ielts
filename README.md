# Free IELTS Prep

When I prepared for my IELTS exam, I could not find a free app with the practice and features I needed, so I built one. This is the free public edition, for any student who cannot pay for expensive courses.

**Use it:** https://taingayting.github.io/free-ielts/

## What is inside

- A day-by-day study plan built from your exam date
- Listening and Reading: links to free official practice tests, and a score log that turns your score into a band
- Writing: a timed editor with word count and a self-check list, plus every Task 1 and Task 2 type with a plan and a model answer
- Speaking: Part 1 questions, Part 2 cards with a 1 + 2 minute timer, and a voice recorder
- Vocabulary flashcards by level, with spaced review and a space for your own Khmer meaning
- 15 grammar lessons with exercises, and an exam guide

Everything is saved in your own browser. Use Settings → Download backup to keep a copy.

This edition contains no Cambridge books, tests or audio. The lessons and word lists were written for this app with AI help.
